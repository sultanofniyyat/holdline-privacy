How to play

Drag left and right to steer your squad down the street. Run through the gates that grow your squad and avoid the ones that shrink it; every gate does exactly what it says. Your soldiers shoot by themselves; get as many as you can to the end to beat the horde and the boss. Powers charge up during a level; tap one in the bottom-left corner to use it.

Your progress

Your levels, squad, gems and purchases are saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.

Purchases

Changed phones or reinstalled the game? Open the Shop, scroll to the bottom and tap "Restore purchases". Your one-time items (No Ads, Full Arsenal, Starter Pack, gold guns and drone) and a running Commander Pass come back. Gems and Season Passes are used up when bought and cannot be restored.

To cancel the Commander Pass: iPhone Settings, tap your name, Subscriptions, Commander Pass, Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the week ends. For a refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads and privacy

Full-screen ads stop for good with No Ads or the Full Arsenal, and while the Commander Pass is running. Reward ads are always your choice. See the Privacy Policy for how ads use your information.
