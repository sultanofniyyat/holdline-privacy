HOLDLINE – Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026 (version 1.0)

HOLDLINE: Zombie Survival is a squad runner game for iPhone by Sultan of Niyyat. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: we do not collect any personal information ourselves. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

Information we collect

None. HOLDLINE has no accounts and no sign-in, and we run no servers that receive your information. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, precise location, photos, camera or microphone.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

HOLDLINE is free to play and shows ads from Google AdMob: full-screen ads between levels and events (never during a level), an ad now and then when you come back to the game, and optional "watch an ad" rewards such as double pay, a continue or free gems. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device type and settings, your IP address (used for an approximate location), how you interact with ads, and app diagnostics.

Google may also use your device's advertising identifier to show ads that fit your interests, but only if you choose "Allow" when the game asks whether it may track you. You can change this at any time in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking. If you do not allow tracking, you still see ads, just less personalised ones.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game first asks for your consent using Google's consent form. You can change your choices later with the "Privacy choices" link at the bottom of the game's Shop.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

You can buy optional items in the game: the Commander Pass (a weekly subscription with a 3-day free trial), No Ads, the Full Arsenal, the Starter Pack, gold guns and drone, Season Passes and gem packs. Payments are handled by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details. The game only learns which items you bought, so it can give them to you. The game uses Unity's in-app purchasing system to talk to the App Store; it passes on the purchase (which item, when) but nothing that identifies you. "Restore purchases" at the bottom of the Shop brings your one-time items and a running Commander Pass back on a new phone.

The Commander Pass renews automatically each week until you cancel. You can cancel at any time, at least 24 hours before the end of the current week, in your iPhone's Settings under your name, Subscriptions.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your levels and stars, your squad, upgrades and base, your gems and credits, event progress, your settings and which purchases you own on your own device, so you can carry on where you left off. We do not receive this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

HOLDLINE is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, for example because a future version adds a new feature, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date before that version of the game is released.
